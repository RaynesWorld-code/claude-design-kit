## 0. Core principles

### 0.1 Phone first
- The phone version is the real design; desktop is the adaptation. Design at 375–390px wide first.
- Tap targets at least 48×48px. Body text never below 16px. Nothing scrolls sideways at 360px.
- Main actions within thumb reach, in the lower half of the screen.

### 0.2 Easy to understand
- Plain words. If a technical word is needed, explain it in the same sentence.
- Every service answers up front: what do I get, how long, how much, what do I need to do?
- A clear next step on every screen.
- The fun never blocks the way: anything animated can also be reached by an ordinary link or button.
