## 1. Core principles

### 1.1 Seen where people see it
- Everything is judged at the size and place people actually meet it: a website on a phone, a logo at favicon size and on a sign, a post in the feed.
- Phone first for anything on screen.

### 1.2 Easy to understand
- Plain words. If a technical word is needed, explain it in the same sentence.
- One idea per post, page section or slide.
- A clear next step wherever there's something to do.

## 11. Don'ts
[Copied from CLAUDE.md at the start; this is the full list and grows over time.]
- ❌ Don't use stock photos, stock icons or emoji as decoration.
- ❌ Don't make up stats, testimonials, reviews or client logos.
- ❌ Website: don't use the "small label, big headline, one sentence, two buttons" hero block, or a grid of rounded cards with an icon, title and two lines.
- ❌ Logo: don't use swooshes, globes, lightbulbs or initials in a circle; don't put a free icon next to a font.
- ❌ Brand pack: don't hand over a logo on a white page and call it a brand.
- ❌ Social: don't make it look like a Canva template; no walls of text.
