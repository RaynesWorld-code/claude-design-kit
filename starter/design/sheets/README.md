# Sheets

Stills of designs, one sheet per round of options. Name each sheet with a letter (A, B, C… then AA, AB…) and label the options on it (A, B, C, D).

Keep rejected sheets: they're ideas for later. Record what was picked and why in `docs/design-system.md`.

The scripts save here too:
- `node design/shoot.js` — websites (phone and desktop) and social posts (`--preset social`).
- `node design/logo-check.js` — the logo at every size, on light, dark and colour.
- `node design/feed-check.js` — posts as they look in the feed and on the profile grid.
