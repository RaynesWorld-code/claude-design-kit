# One round of polish

Repeat this until there's nothing left to fix. Three rounds is the minimum for anything that might become the bar.

---

Screenshot [the page or section] at phone size with `design/shoot.js`, then desktop. Open the pictures and look closely, as if you were going to be judged on it.

List every flaw you can find, however small: stray marks, glare, things floating or not sitting on anything, wrong scale, uneven spacing, text cut off or crowded, wording that's off or not in the owner's voice, anything that looks default, generic or like a kit. Compare it against the bar in `CLAUDE.md` (or the references, if there's no bar yet).

Fix all of them, screenshot again, and check each fix in the new pictures. Then show me the before and after and tell me what, if anything, still feels basic.
