# One round of polish

Repeat this until there's nothing left to fix. Three rounds is the minimum for anything that might become the bar.

---

Render [the piece] the way people will see it: a website page at phone size with `design/shoot.js`, then desktop; a logo with `design/logo-check.js`; posts at their exact size and then with `design/feed-check.js`; print items at real size. Open the pictures and look closely, as if you were going to be judged on it.

List every flaw you can find, however small: stray marks, things floating or not sitting on anything, wrong scale, uneven spacing, wobbly curves or stray points, text cut off, crowded or too small to read at real size, wording that's off or not in the owner's voice, anything that looks default, generic, like a template or like an AI image. Compare it against the bar in `CLAUDE.md` (or the references, if there's no bar yet).

Fix all of them, render again, and check each fix in the new pictures. Then show me the before and after and tell me what, if anything, still feels basic.
