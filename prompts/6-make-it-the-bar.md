# Make a page the bar

Use this once a page is genuinely right.

---

[Page or section] is now the bar. Put its path in "The bar" in `CLAUDE.md`, and write down in `docs/design-system.md` what makes it good (be specific: the materials, the light, the type, the details). Record it in "Decided along the way" and the changelog.

Then go through every other page, screenshot each one, and list where each falls short of the bar. Don't fix anything yet; show me the list so we can choose the order.
