# Explore directions

Use this for anything new or big: a website page or section, a brand pack item, a post template. (For a logo, use `7-logo.md`.)

---

We're designing [the piece]. Read `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/design-system.md` first.

Draw 3–4 **genuinely different** directions as stills: different layouts and ideas, not one layout in four colours. Each must fit the idea in the rulebook, use real content (no lorem ipsum), use the brand (logo, colours, type) if it's already set, and avoid everything in the Don'ts.

Build them in `design/src/` and render them at the size people will see them (`design/shoot.js`: phone for a website, the post size for social, real size for print). Put them side by side on one sheet in `design/sheets/` with the next free sheet letter, labelled A to D. Look at the real renders yourself before showing me, and fix anything broken first.

Show me the sheet with one line on each option. Say which you'd pick and why, but let me choose.
