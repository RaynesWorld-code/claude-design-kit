# Explore directions for a page or section

Use this for anything new or big: a page, a section, a main component.

---

We're designing [the piece]. Read `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/design-system.md` first.

Draw 3–4 **genuinely different** directions as stills: different layouts and ideas, not one layout in four colours. Each must fit the idea in the rulebook, use real content (no lorem ipsum), and avoid everything in the Don'ts.

Build them as HTML in `design/src/`, screenshot them at phone size with `design/shoot.js`, and put them side by side on one sheet in `design/sheets/` with the next free sheet letter, labelled A to D. Look at the real screenshots yourself before showing me, and fix anything broken first.

Show me the sheet with one line on each option. Say which you'd pick and why, but let me choose.
