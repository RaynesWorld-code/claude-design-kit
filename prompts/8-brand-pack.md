# Make a brand pack

Use this once the logo, colours and type are settled.

---

We're making the brand pack. Read `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/design-system.md` first.

1. **Ask me** which things the business actually needs the brand on (business cards, letterhead, email signature, signage, van, uniform, packaging, social profile, anything else) and whether anything will be printed.
2. **Guidelines first.** Build the guidelines as HTML pages in `brand/guidelines/`, in the order in the CLAUDE.md Brand pack section: the idea, logo (with each version, clear space, smallest size and what not to do, shown as pictures), colour (HEX, RGB, CMYK, Pantone if needed), type (with where to get the fonts and their licences, and a fallback), imagery, voice (with before-and-after examples).
3. **In use.** Draw each item at its real size in `design/src/brand/`, using the brand rules. Print items: 3mm bleed, text inside a 5mm safe margin, CMYK. Explore 2–3 directions for the first item (usually the business card), let me pick, then make the rest match.
4. **Check everything as a real render.** Every guidelines page and every item, closely, with a polish round on each. Nothing goes in the pack that hasn't been looked at.
5. **Export.** Guidelines as one PDF. Print items as PDF at real size. Everything the business needs in `brand/`, named plainly (e.g. `logo-full-colour.svg`, `business-card-front.pdf`). Write a short `brand/README.md` saying what each file is for.
